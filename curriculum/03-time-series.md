# S03 — Time Series

S03 introduces time-indexed data, classical forecasting models, and machine-learning workflows for time series.

## Curriculum overview

| Unit  | Name                             |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| BLU04 | Time Series                      |
| BLU05 | Classical Time Series Models     |
| BLU06 | Machine Learning for Time Series |

## BLU04 — Time Series

- Parse and index time data with pandas.
- Select, resample, shift, roll, and aggregate time series.
- Work with multi-indexed time data.
- Identify and reason about trend, cyclical, seasonal, and irregular components.
- Use the Earth-observation extension as optional material.

## BLU05 — Classical Time Series Models

- Decompose time series and inspect autocorrelation and partial autocorrelation.
- Understand stationarity and apply suitable transformations.
- Build and evaluate SARIMAX forecasts.
- Tune classical models with `pmdarima` and reusable pipelines.
- Include exogenous variables and interpret forecast confidence intervals.
- Use the NaN/ACF appendix as optional material.

## BLU06 — Machine Learning for Time Series

- Formulate one-step and multi-step forecasting as regression problems.
- Create differences, rolling statistics, date/time features, and circular encodings.
- Add exogenous features and build preprocessing and feature-engineering workflows.
- Tune and test models without leaking future information.
- Use expanding- and sliding-window cross-validation.
- Estimate and communicate forecast uncertainty.

## Topics outside the core

Anomaly detection, Prophet, exponential-smoothing and GARCH extensions, and deep-learning forecasting are outside the current core curriculum and may be offered as further reading.
